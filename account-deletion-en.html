Account and data deletion

Updated: 9 October 2026

How it works now

Since version 0.2.6 the game keeps a copy of your account on our server, without email or phone number, so you can restore it if you change your phone.

You delete that copy and what is on your device yourself, from inside the game.

What was sent to us as gameplay statistics you ask us to delete by email.

Delete your account from the game

Open Settings, then Account.

Press “Delete my account” (امسح حسابي) and confirm.

This deletes your account from our server and from your device: everything the game stores, your device’s random number and the recovery code.

It also deletes the Lahlouh and the things you bought with it, and these do not come back.

You can restore “Make your character” with “Restore purchases”.

If you want us to delete statistics that were already sent, copy “Your device number” first.

In older versions (before 0.2.6) the button is called “Delete my data” (امسح بياناتي) and deletes only what is on your device.

Delete it from your phone

Leave your online room and close the game first.

If you clear the app’s data from your phone’s settings or uninstall the app, only what is on your device is deleted.

The account copy on the server stays until you delete it with “Delete my account”, or it deletes itself two years after you last used it.

Offloading while keeping application data is not enough.

For browser play, clear the game website’s stored data.

Local deletion does not erase rooms that are still open for other players.

Clear guide statistics only

Open “الدليل”, then “عندك سؤال؟”.

Scroll to your statistics.

Choose “امسح أرقام دليل المصلحة”, then “امسح”.

This resets guide statistics only, leaving other data unchanged.

Ask us to delete what was sent

Gameplay statistics go to PostHog as numbers that are not tied to your name.

What ties them to your device is “Your device number” under Settings, Account, with a “Copy” button.

Email us that number, and we will delete what was sent within a month.

hello@maslahagame.com

Use the subject “Data deletion”.

Write your device number in it, and never include passwords.

Instagram is for news only, not for data requests.

What deletes itself

Crash reports are deleted from the server after 30 days at most.

The account copy is deleted two years after you last used it, and the recovery code stops working after 6 months.

“Friends” data is deleted after 180 days without play.

Online play is not stored on the server, and it is gone when the room closes.

We keep player reports for 90 days.

After deletion we keep the numbers of banned devices and blocked names, to prevent abuse.
