Account and data deletion

Updated: 5 October 2026

How it works now

The game does not ask for a server account, so there is no account to delete.

What is stored on your device, you delete yourself from inside the game.

What was sent to us (gameplay statistics, if you agreed) you ask us to delete by email.

Delete your data from the game

Open Settings, then Account.

Press “Delete my data” (امسح بياناتي) and confirm.

This deletes everything the game stores on your device, and your device’s random number.

It also deletes the Lahlouh and the things you bought with it, and these do not come back.

You can restore “Make your character” with “Restore purchases”.

If you want us to delete statistics that were already sent, copy your device number first.

Delete it from your phone

Leave your online room and close the game first.

If you clear the app’s data from your phone’s settings, the same thing happens.

Or uninstall the application completely.

Offloading while keeping application data is not enough.

For browser play, clear the game website’s stored data.

Local deletion does not erase rooms still open for other players.

Clear guide statistics only

Open “الدليل”, then “عندك سؤال؟”.

Scroll to your statistics.

Choose “امسح أرقام دليل المصلحة”, then “امسح”.

This resets guide statistics only, leaving other data unchanged.

Ask us to delete what was sent

If you agreed to gameplay statistics, numbers that are not tied to your name go to PostHog.

What ties them to your device is “Your device number” under Settings, Account, with a “Copy” button.

Email us that number, and we will delete what was sent within a month.

hello@maslahagame.com

Title the message “Data deletion”.

Include your device number, and do not add passwords.

Instagram is for news only, not for data requests.

What deletes itself

Crash reports, if you turned them on, are deleted from the server after 30 days at most.

Online play is not stored on the server, and it is gone when the room closes.

We keep player reports for 90 days.
