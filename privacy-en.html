Game information

Privacy policy

Updated: 5 October 2026

Without an account

There is no sign-in and no password, no ads, no tracking across apps and no analytics tools other than gameplay statistics.

The game connects to our server in three cases: online play; asking for the “oldest accepted game version” when the home screen opens (with no data from you); and crash reports, if you turn them on.

It also connects to two outside services: PostHog for statistics, if you agree, and RevenueCat for purchases.

The artwork, sounds and fonts are inside the app itself.

What stays on your device

Your name, level, experience, characters, items, settings and gameplay numbers; your running match, if you close the app halfway through; the log of tutorial steps; and a short log of your matches, purchases and app crashes.

All of it stays on your device, except what we say below: online play, crash reports, statistics and purchases.

Online rooms

When you play an online match, we send the game server (Colyseus Cloud in Frankfurt) your name, your character, a random number for your device and the room code, together with your moves and actions.

Your name and character are shown to everyone in the room, so do not use your real name if you prefer not to.

The server passes the rest on only to your friends in the same room.

One player’s phone runs the match for everyone, so it knows the players’ cards.

The server does not save them in a database, and they are gone when the room closes.

And like any server, it sees your internet address (IP address) when you connect.

It also counts rooms and messages and measures memory, processor load and uptime so that the service keeps running.

Estimation with friends

When you create a session or join one with a code, we send the same server your name, the profile picture you chose in the game, your level, a random number for your device and your actions in play.

Your name and picture are shown to your friends in the session.

Each player’s cards go only to that player.

The server saves nothing, and everything is gone when the session closes.

Crash reports

Automatic sending is off from the start.

If you turn it on under Settings › Account › “Send crash reports automatically”, then when the game crashes it sends a report to our server (Colyseus Cloud in Frankfurt) so that we can fix the fault, at most once every two minutes.

It contains your device model, system version, game version, screen size, the latest log events and the error.

It contains no name, no room code, no account and no device number.

And it goes to no one but the hosting company that runs the server.

How a report is kept

If there is no internet, it waits on your device and is sent later.

On the server it is saved in a file that deletes itself after 30 days at most, and we do not store your IP address in it.

As for the “Send a problem report” button, you are the one who chooses who receives it.

Gameplay statistics

We ask about them the first time you open the game, and you can change your mind at any time under Settings › Account.

If you agree, the app sends numbers about your play that are not tied to your name: the result of each procedure (success or failure, its duration and its score), the end of a match (rank, points and character), character purchases and shop activity, first-use steps, your basic settings (sound, font and frame rate), and device performance while you play.

What goes with each number

A random number for your device (not your name or your account), the game version, the device type, the screen size, your level and the number of your characters.

No name, no text you type, no room code, no pictures and no location, and we do not store your IP address.

Who receives them

The numbers go to PostHog (its servers are in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union).

It is only a service provider: it stores them for us for one year and then deletes them, and it neither sells them nor uses them for ads.

If there is no internet they wait on your device and are sent later.

You can turn them off under Settings › Account › “Share gameplay statistics”; they then stop, and we delete what is waiting on your device, together with the device number.

If you want us to delete what was already sent, copy your number from the same page and send it to us, and we will delete it within a month.

Real-money purchases

If you buy Lahlouh or “Make your character”, payment is made through the Apple or Google store and we never see your card details.

The RevenueCat service confirms the purchase and restores “Make your character” for you if you change your device.

It receives the purchase receipt, a random number for the device (not your name) and technical information such as the device type.

RevenueCat is a US company and processes this data in the US, for us only.

This transfer relies on the contractual clauses approved by the European Union.

Motion and microphone

The home screen and procedures such as “Water balance” and “Spin the safe” use your phone’s tilt and motion only during the attempt, after you agree with the “Go ahead, sir” button.

The readings are not recorded and not sent, your location is not used, and touch is always available instead.

The “Blow the dust” procedure measures the loudness of your blowing during the attempt, with your permission; nothing is recorded or sent, and tapping is always available instead.

In online play only the result of the procedure is sent, not the readings.

No chat

Interaction in a match is through ready-made buttons only (a clap, a kiss, money), and the only thing you type is the room code.

Reports

When you report a player, we receive their displayed name, their random number and the reason for the report, and we keep them for 90 days.

We keep the numbers of banned devices for as long as the ban lasts.

Service providers

We use only three companies.

All of them work for us and on our instructions, and are bound to protect your data at the same level as this page or higher.

Colyseus Cloud for the game server (Frankfurt), PostHog for statistics (Frankfurt) and RevenueCat for purchases (US).

Their privacy policies: cloud-prod.colyseus.io/privacy-policy, posthog.com/privacy and revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

The “Delete my data” button in Settings deletes everything the game stores on your device and your device’s random number, together with the Lahlouh and the things you bought with it (these do not come back).

You can restore “Make your character” with “Restore purchases”.

If you clear the app’s data from your phone’s settings, the same thing happens.

And the statistics that were already sent are deleted if you send us your number, as we said above.

Your rights

You have the right to know what we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to object or withdraw your consent at any time.

Withdrawing your consent does not affect what was done before it.

Statistics and crash reports are by your consent; online play and purchases are so that we can give you what you asked for.

Email us at hello@maslahagame.com with the number shown in Settings, and we will reply within a month.

If you are in Europe, you have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

And if you are in Egypt, you have the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Centre.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

If you are under 18, statistics stay off, and you should ask an adult before you buy anything with real money.

And if you find out that your child sent us data, talk to us and we will delete it.

If this changes

If we add something new that is sent from you, we will change this page before that and write the update date at the top.

If the change is big, we will tell you inside the game and ask you again if we need your consent.

Contact

The person responsible for your data is Adel Gamal Mohamed Abdullah (عادل جمال محمد عبد الله), an individual developer in Egypt.

For any question or request about your data, email hello@maslahagame.com.

The game’s Instagram page, maslaha_game, is for news only, not for data requests.

This website

The game’s web page counts visits with PostHog (its servers are in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union) without cookies and without storing your IP address.

We only record which page you opened, which buttons you pressed, your device type and the site you came from, and no names or emails.

When the page asks the game server how many people are playing right now, the server sees your IP address, like any connection.

We respect the “Do Not Track” setting in your browser.
