Game information

Privacy policy

Updated: 24 October 2026

Signing in is optional

Signing in with Google (and on iPhone with Apple too) is optional, and we keep only the account ID, with no email and no name (except the email of a “Make your character” order, below).

If you change your phone, your progress does not move by itself, so keep your recovery code.

There is no password and no phone number, no ads, no tracking across apps and no analytics tools other than gameplay statistics.

The game connects to our server when you play online, when you open “Friends”, for crash reports, for a copy of your account that we keep so you can restore it if your phone is wiped or you change it, and for asking for the “oldest accepted game version”.

That same request also checks whether there is a message from the team or changed game settings, when the home screen opens and every 5 minutes while it is open, with no data from you.

It also connects to outside services: PostHog for statistics, RevenueCat for purchases, Sentry for crashes and Capgo for game updates.

Your connection to our server goes through Cloudflare.

The artwork, sounds and fonts are inside the app itself.

We use the data on this page to run the game, protect it, fix its faults and improve it.

What stays on your device

Your name, level, experience, characters, items, settings and gameplay numbers; your running match, if you close the app halfway through; the log of tutorial steps; a short log of your matches, purchases and app crashes; and the last 20 players you played with (a name and a random number for each).

All of it stays on your device, except what we say below.

Reminders are local on your phone, with your permission, and you can turn them off.

Account copy

We keep a copy of your account on our server: your name, your Lahlouh, the characters and cosmetics you bought, attendance, achievements, missions, your progress in Estimation, your statistics and the list of players you blocked, tied to a random number for your device and a secret kept on your device.

It comes with an 8-character recovery code that you type on a new phone to restore your account; the code keeps working for 6 months after you last opened the game.

It is deleted when you press “Delete my account” (امسح حسابي), or two years after you last used it.

An account with a real-money purchase is not deleted by time; we delete it when you ask.

Invites and anti-abuse

We keep a number derived from your IP address and your device type, not the address itself, only to compare.

It is not sent anywhere and not used for ads.

It is renewed every time you enter the game, and deleted after 7 days or with your account.

If an invite comes from the same phone or network, the owner of the code gets no gift.

Online rooms

When you play an online match, we send the game server (Colyseus Cloud in Frankfurt) your name, your character, a random number for your device and the room code, together with your moves and actions.

Your name and character are shown to everyone in the room.

The server passes the rest on only to your friends in the same room.

One player’s phone runs the match for everyone, so it knows the players’ cards.

The server does not save them in a database, and they are gone when the room closes.

The connection goes through Cloudflare: it sees your internet address (IP address) and passes it to our server, and our server sees it when you connect.

Profile card and version

Along with your name and character we send your profile card: your level, the number of your matches and wins, the date of your first match, the character you play most, your title and your stamps, and what you are wearing.

All of it is shown to everyone in the room.

And the server remembers the last game version you came in with, for up to 90 days.

Estimation with friends

When you create a session or join one with a code, we send the same server your name, the profile picture you chose in the game, your level, a random number for your device and your actions in play.

Your name and picture are shown to your friends in the session.

Each player’s cards go only to that player.

The server saves nothing, and everything is gone when the session closes.

If you play online we also send the number of your games, the percentage of your correct estimates and your rank, and they are shown to your friends in the session.

Crash reports

Automatic sending is on from the start, and you can turn it off under Settings › Account › “Send crash reports automatically”.

When the game crashes it sends a report to our server so that we can fix the fault, at most once every two minutes.

Turning it off stops all sending from the next launch of the game.

It is also sent when your online connection is cut for good (you were disconnected and did not come back, you joined from another device, or you were removed), at most once every 10 minutes.

It contains your device model, system version, game version, screen size, the number of processors and the memory, the latest log events and the error.

It contains no name, no room code, no account and no device number.

The same crash also goes to Sentry (Frankfurt) with the screen where it happened, and it is deleted after 30 days.

If statistics are on, it carries a fixed code that we compute from your device number and that cannot be turned back into the number.

On a phone, Sentry also receives how many times the game was opened and closed, without your name or your player number.

System crashes that close the game are sent the next time it opens, in their original form, without the game’s own cleaning.

It goes to no one but Cloudflare, the hosting company and Sentry.

How a report is kept

If there is no internet, it waits on your device and is sent later.

On the server it is saved in a file that deletes itself after 30 days at most, and we do not store your IP address in it.

As for the “Send a problem report” button, you are the one who chooses who receives it.

And the “Contact us” (كلّمنا) button prepares an email with the report and your device number (if statistics are on), and you are the one who presses send.

Gameplay statistics

On iPhone, and on any phone in Europe, the UK and Switzerland, we ask you the first time you open the game and send nothing before you agree.

In other countries on Android they are on from the start, after you answer “Are you 13 or older?”.

If you are under 13 we send nothing.

We send numbers about your play without your name, but tied to a random number for the installation: the result of each procedure (success or failure, its duration and its score), the end of a match (rank, points and character), character purchases and shop activity, first-use steps, your basic settings (sound, font and frame rate), and device performance while you play.

What goes with each number

A random number for your device (not your name or your account), the game version, the device type, the screen size, the device language, the system version, the font and frame-rate settings, how long the account has existed, your Lahlouh balance, your level and the number of your characters.

No name, no text you type, no room code, no pictures and no location, and we do not store your IP address.

Who receives them

The numbers go to PostHog (its servers are in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union).

It is only a service provider: it stores them for us for one year and then deletes them, and it neither sells them nor uses them for ads.

If there is no internet they wait on your device and are sent later.

You can change your mind at any time under Settings › Account › “Share gameplay statistics”; they then stop, and we delete what is waiting on your device, together with the device number.

If you want us to delete what was already sent, copy your number from the same page and send it to us, and we will delete it within a month.

Real-money purchases

Payment is made through the Apple or Google store and we never see your card details.

The RevenueCat service confirms the purchase and restores “Make your character” for you if you change your device.

It receives the purchase receipt, a random number for the device (not your name) and technical information.

RevenueCat is a US company and processes this data in the US, for us only, and the transfer relies on the contractual clauses approved by the European Union.

RevenueCat also sends us a record of every sale or refund: the product, the price, the country and the store’s transaction number, without your name or your player number.

We use it only for our accounts and taxes.

We keep it for 5 years after the tax return for the year of the sale, because Egyptian tax law requires us to keep our books that long, and then we delete the transaction number.

“Delete my account” does not delete it.

Make your character

We make your character from your pictures with artificial-intelligence tools, and we send the pictures to Nover, whose processor is Runware, so that they make your character.

The character appears in the game, and all players see it and play with it.

In the pictures step there is a separate choice, empty at the start: “I agree that you keep my pictures to develop the game and your other products” (موافق تحتفظوا بصوري لتطوير اللعبة ومنتجاتكم التانية).

If you agree, we keep your pictures until you ask us by email to delete them, and you can take your agreement back.

When you delete your account, we delete your pictures too, even if you agreed to our keeping them.

If you do not agree, we delete them from the chat, the email and Nover within 30 days of delivery, and the order goes ahead as normal.

If you are under 18, we need your parent’s or guardian’s agreement, and we ask for it before we start.

Order email

We collect your email when we prepare your order, even if you do not finish the purchase.

If the purchase is not finished, we delete the email 30 days after we collected it.

If we delivered the order or cancelled it, we delete the email 30 days after the delivery or the cancellation.

If the order is still open, the email stays until the order closes or you delete your account.

An encrypted copy of it stays in the backup for up to 90 days at most.

Motion and microphone

The home screen and procedures such as “Steady the bubble” and “Turn the dial” use your phone’s tilt and motion only during the attempt, after you agree with the “Go ahead, sir” button.

The readings are not recorded and not sent, your location is not used, and touch is always available.

The “Blow the dust” procedure measures the loudness of your blowing during the attempt, with your permission; nothing is recorded or sent, and tapping is always available.

In online play only the result of the procedure is sent.

No chat

Interaction in a match is through 12 ready-made reactions such as a clap, a kiss and money, and what you type is your name, the room code, and an optional note (up to 80 characters) if you report a player.

Friends

If you open “Friends”, we keep on the game server your name, your character, your own friend code, your friends list, your requests and room invitations, without your IP address or device model.

We also keep a scrambled fingerprint of the secret on your device (not the secret itself, and it cannot be undone), and we use it to tie your code to your account.

We delete them if you do not open the game for 180 days, or when you press “Delete my account”.

Reports

When you report a player, we receive their displayed name, their random number, your own device number, the reason for the report and your note if you wrote one, together with the room’s internal number (not the code you enter with) and a scrambled fingerprint of your account secret (not the secret itself), and we keep them for 90 days.

We keep the numbers of banned devices for as long as the ban lasts.

A report sent by email from “Played with” (لعبت معاهم) reaches our email inbox.

The player’s name and number stay there only to review the report.

Service providers

Colyseus Cloud for the game server (Frankfurt), PostHog for statistics (Frankfurt) and RevenueCat for purchases (US).

Sentry for crashes (Frankfurt), and Cloudflare for passing your connection to the server (a US company with a global network).

These work for us and on our instructions, and are bound to protect your data at the same level as this page or higher.

These providers, Cloudflare included, keep operating logs for a short time (such as the time, the link, your internet address, your approximate location and your device type), so the service runs and we can find faults and stop abuse.

Capgo, for game updates, is a company working under its own terms, not on our instructions, and we cannot ask it to delete your device number.

Capgo sees the app version, your phone’s system and its version, where you installed the game from, a fixed random number for your device (not your name or your account; on iPhone it stays even if you delete the game and install it again), and your internet address, like any website.

No statistics and no crashes go to it.

And in “Make your character”, Nover and its processor Runware receive your pictures.

Their privacy policies: cloud-prod.colyseus.io/privacy-policy, posthog.com/privacy, revenuecat.com/privacy, sentry.io/privacy, cloudflare.com/privacypolicy, capgo.app/privacy, nover.studio/privacy and runware.ai/privacy-policy.

And if you link your account, Apple or Google confirm your sign-in under their own policies: apple.com/legal/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

The game server counts rooms and messages and measures memory, processor load and uptime so that the service keeps running.

Deleting your account

The “Delete my account” button under Settings › Account deletes your account from our server and from your device: everything the game stores on your device, the statistics number, the recovery code and the link to Apple or Google, together with the Lahlouh and the things you bought with it (these do not come back).

You can restore “Make your character” with “Restore purchases”.

If the account is linked to Apple, when you delete it or unlink, we ask Apple to cancel the link, and we repeat the request up to 3 times if it fails.

This happens when you delete from inside the game on iPhone and confirm with Apple.

If you close Apple’s sheet, or delete by email, we cannot cancel the link from our side: stop it yourself in iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple › Qaadet Maslaha › Delete.

If you clear the app’s data from your phone’s settings, only what is on your device is deleted, and the account copy on the server stays until you delete it.

If you do not have the game, email us with the subject “Delete my account” (امسح حسابي) and your recovery code, and we will delete the account within 30 days and reply.

And the statistics that were already sent are deleted if you send us your number.

What remains after deletion

We remove your device number from the reports you filed, and the reports stay without a number.

We keep the numbers of banned devices, blocked names and the reports filed against the device, to prevent abuse.

An encrypted copy of your account may stay in the backup for up to 90 days after deletion, and it cannot be opened without the key.

If we restore a backup on the current server, we replay the deletion of every account we recorded as deleted.

Your rights

You have the right to know what we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to object or withdraw your consent at any time.

Withdrawing your consent does not affect what was done before it.

Statistics work as explained in “Gameplay statistics” (and you can stop them); crash reports are so that we can fix the game; online play, purchases and the account copy are so that we can give you what you asked for.

Email us at hello@maslahagame.com with the number shown in Settings, and we will reply within a month.

If you are in Europe, you have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

And if you are in Egypt, you have the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Centre.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13.

We ask whether you are 13 or older, and keep your answer (yes or no) on your device only, not your birth year.

If you answer no, we turn off statistics, real-money purchases, friend requests and public rooms.

And we collect only what the game needs to work: your account copy on our server (your play name, Lahlouh and progress, without email or phone), playing in a private room with a code.

Also crash reports if the switch is on, Capgo’s number, and your internet address and short operating logs at Cloudflare.

And your “Friends” record (your name, character and friend code) if you join an online room or open “Friends”.

If you are under 18, ask an adult before you buy anything with real money.

And if you answered in an older version that you were under 18, statistics stay off.

And if you find out that your child sent us data, talk to us and we will delete it.

If this changes

If we add something new that is sent from you, we will change this page before that and write the update date at the top.

If the change is big, we will tell you inside the game and ask you again if we need your consent.

Contact

For any question or request about your data, email hello@maslahagame.com.

The providers: who sees what

These are the same companies as above, one by one.

Colyseus Cloud in Frankfurt: the game server.

It sees your name, your character, your device’s random number, the room code and your moves, and your internet address while you are connected.

Your account copy there is deleted with the “Delete my account” button, or two years after you last used it, except an account with a real-money purchase: that one stays until you ask us to delete it.

PostHog in Frankfurt: gameplay statistics with a random number, without your name or your internet address.

They are kept for one year and then deleted.

To have them deleted sooner, send your number to hello@maslahagame.com and we will delete them within a month.

RevenueCat in the US: the purchase receipt, a random buyer number and the device type.

It keeps them so that it can restore “Make your character” on any phone.

“Delete my account” does not reach it.

To have it deleted, email us and we delete it by hand.

Sentry in Frankfurt: the crash report, the screen where it happened and the device type.

It deletes itself after 30 days, and you can stop sending under Settings › Account; the full stop starts from the next launch.

Cloudflare: passes the game’s connection to our server.

It sees your internet address and passes it to our server.

It keeps a short log of each request for us (such as the time, the link, your internet address, your approximate location and your device type) so we can find faults, and it may keep other logs under its own policy.

Capgo: the app version, your phone’s system, where you installed the game from, a fixed random number for your device and your internet address.

Capgo is a company with its own terms, not working on our instructions.

Capgo’s policy does not say how long it keeps them.

We cannot delete that number: “Delete my account” does not reach it, and on iPhone it stays even if you delete the game.

It is not tied to your name or your account.

Nover and its processor Runware: your pictures, if you bought “Make your character”.

Details in “Your photos and receipt”.

Your photos and receipt

When you send us your photos for “Make your character”, we send them to Nover, whose processor is Runware, so that they make your character.

We use them for “Make your character” only, unless you tick the keep-photos box: then also to develop the game and our other products.

If you do not agree to our keeping your photos, we delete them from the chat, the email and Nover within 30 days of delivery.

If you agree, we keep them until you ask us to delete them.

When you delete your account, we delete your photos too, even if you agreed to our keeping them.

You can ask us to delete your photos, the copy of your purchase receipt at RevenueCat and your buyer number by emailing hello@maslahagame.com.

This website

The game’s web page counts visits with PostHog (its servers are in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union) without cookies and without storing your IP address.

We only record which page you opened, which buttons you pressed, your device type and the site you came from, and no names or emails.

When the page asks the game server how many people are playing right now, the server sees your IP address, like any connection.

We respect the “Do Not Track” setting in your browser.

Backups

Every 6 hours, starting at 3:10 am Cairo time (3:10 am, 9:10 am, 3:10 pm and 9:10 pm), the server copies all the player data it stores (not the crash reports).

That is accounts, recovery codes, purchase links, Apple or Google sign-in links, invite codes, the purchase log, gifts, friends, reports, bans, blocked names, daily operating numbers, the admin log with its notes, and the “Make your character” orders with the buyer’s email.

The copy is compressed and locked with AES-256 encryption.

The key is not with GitHub.

It is in the hosting settings and in the owner’s password manager only.

One file per day is saved in a private GitHub repository.

GitHub holds encrypted bytes that it cannot read.

It is a provider for these backups only, and its privacy statement is at docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

The server keeps the newest 30 days and removes older files.

If you delete your account, its encrypted copy stays in the latest backups for up to 30 days, and in the repository’s history for 90 days at most.

The owner cleans that history once a month, and it cannot be opened without the key.

A restore on the current server replays our deletion log, so an account we recorded as deleted does not come back.

Admin sees the rooms

The owner’s dashboard lists the rooms that are live right now.

For each room it shows the game, the room code (private rooms too), public or private, lobby or match, and the start time.

It also shows the players’ names and game versions, and who hosts.

It refreshes every 5 seconds from the server’s memory, and it is not stored.

The owner can close a room or remove a player, and must type a reason.

For one year the log keeps the room code, the game, the room’s state, the number of players and the reason.

For a removal it also keeps the player’s name and the first 12 characters of their number.

The owner can also join a live room from his own phone as “the manager” (مدير المصلحة), and everyone in the room sees that he arrived.

This is to stop abuse and to keep the service running.

If you support us

In the “Support the game” section you can send us a screenshot of your transfer in a message to maslaha_game on Instagram.

We receive that message and screenshot on Instagram.

We use them for one thing only: to confirm that the transfer arrived and reply to you, or to send the money back if you sent it by mistake.

We delete them after we reply or send the money back, or after 90 days at most.

Support is voluntary and gives nothing inside the game.

Instagram’s own policy also applies to the message: privacycenter.instagram.com/policy.
