About the maker

I’m Adel Gamal, a creative director with 16 years in advertising. Not a programmer.

Every game I played felt like it was missing something. So I thought: why not make the one I actually want to play? The idea, the rules, the characters, the clerks, the jokes: all mine. The code? All AI. We worked shoulder to shoulder: I’m the brains, it’s the muscle.
