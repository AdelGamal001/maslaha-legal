Terms of use

Updated: 9 October 2026

About the game

Qaadet Maslaha is an entertainment game by Adel Gamal Mohamed Abdullah (عادل جمال محمد عبد الله), an individual developer in Egypt, and you play it as it is.

We keep developing it, so the rules and numbers may change from one release to the next.

The two currencies

“Happy New Year” (كل سنة وانت طيب) is the match currency and it ends with the match.

Lahlouh (لحلوح) is a permanent balance that you spend in the shop.

Neither has any cash value, neither can be turned into money, and neither can be sold or bought outside the game.

Real-money purchases

You can buy Lahlouh or “Make your character” from the shop inside the game.

Payment is made through the Apple or Google store and under their terms, and the price is shown before you confirm.

Lahlouh is stored on your device with a copy on our server, and comes back with the recovery code if you change your device.

It does not come back if you delete your account.

“Restore purchases” restores “Make your character” only.

A refund is requested from Apple or Google under their terms, and this does not affect any right you have under the law of your country.

If you are under 18, buy with the permission of your parent or guardian.

Rooms

Whoever creates the room chooses its settings.

Any player can leave the match at any time with the “Exit the match” (اطلع من الماتش) button, and an unfinished match earns no points.

The server does not check every gameplay move.

Before online play

Your name is shown to everyone in the room.

Names and behaviour that include swearing, insults or harassment are forbidden.

When you enter online play and press “Agree”, you agree to these terms.

Respect

Do not choose a name or do anything that includes swearing, insults, harassment or impersonating someone.

You can block any player: you will not see their messages or emojis, and we will not put you with them in quick play.

And you can report them with the “Report” button.

We review reports within 24 hours, and we may change an offensive name or ban the reported device from online play.

If many players from different rooms report the same person, their name or online play is locked automatically for a day until we review it.

Game rights

The game, its characters, cards, artwork and texts belong to the project owner or are used with the permission of their owners, and are available for personal play only.

Copying, republishing or selling them is forbidden.

Real people

Some characters (those carrying the “based on a real person” stamp) are based on real people who agreed to it.

The artwork is a caricature, for fun, and not a true description of them.

Most of the game’s artwork and some of its sounds were made with artificial-intelligence tools.

The Instagram button on a character opens that person’s account outside the game, and the other Instagram buttons open the game’s page.

If any of them withdraws consent, we will remove the character in the nearest update, and if you had bought it, we will give you its price back in Lahlouh.

No warranty

To the extent the law allows, there is no guarantee that the game will run without faults or that your account will not be lost: we keep a second copy so that we can restore it, but we do not guarantee it.

This does not cancel any right you have as a consumer under the law of your country.

These terms are governed by Egyptian law.

If we change these terms, we will write the change here with its date, and if it is big we will tell you inside the game.
