How it plays

  1. Get your file

    Each match deals you a random task. Every request needs different papers.

  2. Grab a spot in line

    Desks are fewer than players. Each clerk accepts only a few.

  3. Win the right cards

    A card shop opens between rounds. One good card flips the match.

  4. Keep your nerve

    You have a body bar and a nerves bar. Empty either, and you’re out.

Win your way
